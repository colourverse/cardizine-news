Find a story for the next card

Pick a day or a range of days and press Find stories. The finder collects:

  • Wikipedia — that day's Current events page, item by item. No key needed.
  • The Guardian — culture, books, film, music, art, stage, TV, science, environment, technology, world and UK news. Needs a free key.
  • New York Times — the month's archive, filtered to arts, books, culture, science, climate, world and US news. Needs a free developer key.
  • BBC — the BBC has no public archive API, so each day gets links to the BBC News front page and sections as they looked that day (Wayback Machine), plus a Google search of the BBC for that date.

Sport and party politics are hidden unless they reach culture or general news. Star stories to add them to your shortlist. Add your API keys in Settings.
